# Growth notes (why views are low, and what we change)
Data from the owner: Ep 4 got **7 views on YouTube, 233 on Instagram**. I can't see the analytics, the retention graph or the actual video, so this is diagnosis from the scripts and from how short-form feeds work. Treat it as hypotheses to test, not facts.

## Most likely causes (biggest first)
1. **Every episode is written for people who saw the last one, and almost nobody has.** Feeds show a Reel or Short to strangers first. Ep 4 opened on "91% of you chose B" and characters a stranger has never met. A cold viewer has no reason to stay and swipes in 1-2 seconds, so the platform stops showing it.
2. **85 seconds is long for a new account.** The feed judges a short largely on how much of it people watch. A stranger watching 20 s of 85 s is 24%. The same 20 s of 55 s is 36%.
3. **The vote acknowledgment at frame 0** ("Most of you chose...") tells a stranger "this is part 4 of something you missed".
4. **YouTube at 7 views** usually means the Short was barely tested: a brand-new channel, no early engagement, and a weak first second. Also check it was uploaded as a Short (vertical, under 3 minutes, `#shorts` in the title or description helps on new channels).
5. **Unknown: the video itself.** The animation, voice and pacing may be the problem rather than the script. I haven't seen a render.

## What changes from Ep 5
- **Cold-viewer rule:** the first 3 seconds must make sense to someone who never saw any episode: a striking image + one line that explains itself ("Imagine your little brother falls asleep... and three thousand of them kneel to him."). The vote acknowledgment moves to about 4 s.
- **Length 55-65 s** while the account is small. Go back to 85 s only if retention says people stay.
- **Show "PART 5" small in the HUD,** so curious strangers know there's more and go to the profile.
- **Every episode's caption** starts with a one-line hook for strangers, then "Part 5. Start at Part 1 (pinned)".
- **Pin Part 1** on both platforms.
