# Series arc: where HOURS AWAKE is going (regardless of votes)
Votes change the road and who survives. They never change the truth underneath. This file is that truth. Every episode reveals one piece of it, whichever branch we're on. Never state these answers on screen before their episode.

## 1. The angle in one line
It isn't a zombie story. It's a story about **what you'd sacrifice for your little brother**, wrapped in a mystery about sleep. And the calm voice asking "what would you do?" is the villain.

Every episode delivers **one loss** (emotional) and **one answer** (mystery). The scale keeps growing: you → your brother → the group → the city → the world.

## 2. The hidden truth (what's really going on)
1. **The Note is a recording of YOU.** As a child you were `SUBJECT 0` in a sleep study run from the basement clinic (`B1 · SLEEP STUDY`): the attempt to make a person who never needs to sleep. The study recorded your brainwave and turned it into a sound. Forty-one hours before Ep 1, it was broadcast to every phone.
   - Explains: the barcode mark behind your ear (Ep 1), the microsleep that didn't turn you (Ep 2), 54 hours awake with your eyes shut and still not asleep (Ep 4), the cot with your name (Ep 4).
2. **The Sleepers aren't dead. They're all dreaming the same dream**, and it's your dream. That's why they hunt whoever is awake: an awake mind is a crack in the dream.
3. **Ollie was `SUBJECT 1`.** Same family, same brain, a younger and gentler copy. He's the only person who can sleep *inside* the dream and still be himself. So when he sleeps, the dream treats him as its center: they **kneel** (Ep 1, 2, 4). Awake, he's just prey.
4. **The narrator is the dream itself** (the "virus"). It survives only while you're awake. Every "Stay with me" and "I'll stay with you" was it keeping you awake so it could live. **The cure is you falling asleep.** The narrator has been comforting you away from the cure the whole time.
5. **The radio's "help comes in seven days" is a loop**, made by the same study. The breath in the radio static is the narrator's breath (planted since Ep 1). "Dawn Camp" is the study's main facility, not a refuge.

## 3. Fixed milestones (happen in every branch, by different roads)
| Ep | Fixed reveal | What changes by branch |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | **You are Subject 0.** (Files, the cot, a childhood photo with Maya in it) | Who is with you; whether Ollie is awake or asleep |
| 6 | **The radio is a loop, Dawn Camp is the lab, and the dream needs you awake.** | Which ally dies; whether Reyes redeems himself or becomes the human villain |
| 7 | **The narrator drops the act.** Final choice: **sleep** (the dream ends, the world wakes up, Ollie wakes up, but you may never wake again) or **stay awake** (keep yourself and Ollie; the world stays asleep forever) | How much you still have to lose when you make it |

Finale line (fixed): every Sleeper's mouth opens at once and the narrator speaks through all of them: **"I never warned you. I was keeping you company."**

## 4. Character arcs
- **Ollie:** from a silent kid nobody cared about (Ep 1-2) to the one person the dream obeys. In "asleep" branches he drifts toward being the dream's ruler, gentle and terrifying (a possible antagonist you still love). In "awake" branches he's your partner, and his crayon notes become the clues. Fixed: he never speaks aloud until the very last episode. One word. Saved for the finale.
- **Reyes:** the survival math man ("IT WAS HIM OR ALL OF US"). Two possible ends, decided by votes: **redemption by mirror**, where he becomes the bait himself, the job he forced on Ollie; or **the human villain**, who wants to use Ollie as a weapon to command the horde ("Where's the kid? ...Good." in Ep 5A). Either way he's right just often enough that comments argue about him.
- **Maya:** she knew because she *worked* there. She was a night nurse at the sleep study and recognized your mark in Ep 1. Her guilt is the series' quiet engine. Fixed: she's the one who explains Subject 0, and it costs her (in most branches she doesn't survive Ep 6).
- **DJ:** 16, the hope. He believes the radio. Discovering that "help in seven days" is a loop breaks him (Ep 6). He gave you his headphones (Ep 4), and that pays off: noise-canceling headphones are how you survived the Note at Hour 0, and they're what can shield someone from it again in the finale.

## 5. How the votes work from here
- Votes choose **who you lose and how**, never whether the truth comes out.
- Every vote stays "save yourself vs. risk it for someone you love", but the "someone" widens: Ollie → the group → strangers → the whole world in Ep 7.
- Every episode opens with "Imagine" + last episode's final moment + "Most of you chose X", and somewhere inside it shows the cost of that choice, so voters feel it was theirs.

## 6. After Story 1: the channel's long game
- Story 2 is the demon story (seed: *The Dimmers*, from CLAUDE.md 3.6). New city, new cast, new rules.
- **The one thing that carries over is the narrator.** Same voice, same breath sample, same "I'll stay with you." At the end of Story 1 it says it will find someone else to keep it company. In Story 2, viewers slowly realize it's the same voice. The channel's real villain is the voice asking "what would you do?"
- This gives the account a reason to binge every story and to rewatch old episodes for the breath clue.

## 7. Guardrails
- Keep the explanation small and physical: files, a photo, a cot, a sound. No science lectures.
- No answer arrives without a cost in the same episode.
- If a vote would break a fixed milestone, change the road, not the milestone.
