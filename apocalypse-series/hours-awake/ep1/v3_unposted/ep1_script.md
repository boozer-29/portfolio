## 1. Why this version works
- **Hook = the ending.** Frame 0 is the roof, the helicopter and the choice. The viewer knows the stakes in 3 s and stays to see how you got there. The last shot loops back to frame 0.
- **The rules turn on you.** Rule 3 says "if they hum, run". At the twist the one humming is your brother.
- **Real dilemma.** Neither option is safe. A = save billions, abandon an 8-year-old who just squeezed your hand. B = keep your brother, let humanity's only cure die on a roof.
- **Two twists that flip the scene.** Mid: the "cure" the helicopter wants is you. Big: the horde isn't coming for the noise, it's answering Ollie.

## 5. Where each vote leads (branches do NOT rejoin)
Only the winning branch is built, so a full divergence costs nothing extra.

**A · TAKE THE SEAT → Ep 2 "Altitude" (Hour 42)**
You leave. From the air you watch Ollie, still asleep, stand up on the roof, and the whole horde bows to him. Then you notice the pilot hasn't blinked: he has been flying asleep the entire time. Story becomes: you, the cure, inside a crashing "rescue", and Ollie becoming the Sleepers' leader below (your brother turns into the series antagonist).
Ep 2 vote example: wake the pilot (he might turn) / take the controls yourself (you've never flown).

**B · STAY WITH OLLIE → Ep 2 "Ground Zero" (Hour 42)**
The helicopter leaves without you. The stairwell fills with Sleepers; they reach the roof door and stop. They kneel. Ollie, asleep, raises one hand, and all of them freeze. DJ's radio: the helicopter went down 3 km away (pilot's closed eyes pays off). Story becomes: a siege where your sleeping brother is the only shield, and he gets weaker every time you try to wake him.
Ep 2 vote example: wake Ollie (lose the shield) / let him sleep (lose your brother to the dream).
