# HOURS AWAKE: Episode 2 / 7, "Thirty Seconds" (Branch A: THE ROOF), v2 continuous cut
Follows the posted Episode 1 v2 "The Note" (ended: thousands of Sleepers kneel, open their eyes and stare up at YOUR window; vote A ROOF / B BASEMENT; A won).

**Directing principle:** one continuous scene, like a film. Every event is caused by the one before it. Only dead time (10 quiet hours) is skipped; everything that happens while the plot moves is shown step by step. The plank is introduced as a door brace, so taking it to escape is what dooms the door.

## 1. The chain of cause and effect
1. You run → your footsteps echo → that sound breaks the kneeling circle → they smash the lobby glass and fill the stairwell.
2. You reach the roof → Reyes slams the door → it starts shaking → you search the roof for weight → cinder blocks + a scaffold plank → you brace the door. (Reyes glances at the 3 m gap to the next building while you wedge the plank. Seed.)
3. The door holds → the banging stops → they just hum. Ten hours of nothing (skipped).
4. Exhaustion → Ollie fights sleep in your arms → you fight it too → you lose: a 30-second microsleep.
5. You wake → arms empty → the door is still braced (he didn't go through it) → his beanie on the fire-escape ladder (he climbed down the outside).
6. Maya's map → one dot moving down inside the building → CCTV stills: Ollie asleep, Sleepers kneeling as he passes.
7. That answers Ep 1: they were staring at HIM. And the reason the door held for 10 hours: he was on the roof.
8. He reaches the basement → the banging restarts, harder → the blocks slide → the only way off the roof is across the gap → the only thing long enough is the plank holding the door → Reyes rips it off and bridges the gap → now the door is failing for real.
9. Dilemma on a clock: cross the plank (A), or go down the fire escape after Ollie (B). Either way the door falls in seconds.
