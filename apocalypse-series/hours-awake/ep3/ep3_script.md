## 1. Pre-writing checklist (STORY_CRAFT.md)
- **Relationship ritual:** Ollie can't speak (one sound and they come), so he talks in crayon. Three taps on your hand mean "I'm still awake." Set up warm in flashback; returns over the walkie at the worst moment.
- **Character in 12 seconds:** he writes R backwards ("BЯAVE", the name of his dinosaur); gives you the bigger half of the last granola bar; draws you with a cape captioned "MY BROTHER NEVER LEAVES"; clips a toy walkie to your hoodie: "IF WE GET LOST I'LL TAP".
- **Split-second moment:** the plank kick. Reyes kicks it into the gap a split second after your foot leaves it. Replayed later in slow motion once we know he planned everything.
- **Guilty vote:** last vote made the audience leave him. This episode first comforts them ("He chose this. It's not your fault.") and then rips the comfort away: he didn't choose; he was sent, and he's still waiting. The new A asks them to abandon him a second time, knowingly.
- **Twist through a person, not a screen:** the backwards R in a child's handwriting; Reyes's stained fingers; three taps.
- **Quiet last image:** the walkie in your palm, its red light blinking with each tap, next to the cape drawing.
- **2 AM line:** "He thinks you're coming."
- **Half the narration is feeling/thought:** "He chose this. It's not your fault." / "You can't run on a plank." / "He thinks you're coming."
- **Unanswered on purpose:** did Maya and DJ know? (They look away.) And the narrator told you in Ep 2 that Ollie was asleep. He wasn't. Why did the narrator lie? (Narrator-is-the-virus plant.)

## 2. Influences (structure only, nothing copied)
- *The Last of Us* (HBO, ep. 3): love told through short, intimate flashbacks inside a survival story → our 12-second crayon flashback.
- *The Road*: a parent and child's tiny private rituals as the emotional spine → the three taps.
- *A Quiet Place*: a family communicating without sound → crayon notes as Ollie's only voice.
- *The Walking Dead* (Shane): the trusted protector who makes the brutal survival math behind your back → Reyes.
- *Train to Busan*: survival vs. selflessness, decided in seconds → the vote.

## 3. Chain of cause and effect
1. You chose A → you're on the plank, five floors up, as the door behind you breaks.
2. The plank creaks (rule 2) → the Sleepers burst through (rule 3) → you can't run → Reyes drags you over.
3. The Sleepers flood the old roof → stop at the edge → turn and go back down, toward Ollie (they follow him).
4. Reyes kicks the plank into the gap (no way back, and nobody can follow).
5. Safe, exhausted (rule 1) → you grip Ollie's dinosaur to stay awake → flashback: who Ollie is.
6. You find a crayon note in your pocket, signed Ollie: he chose to lead them away → relief (and the narrator soothes you).
7. You re-read it → "BRAVE" is spelled correctly → Ollie always wrote the R backwards (shown in the flashback) → he didn't write it.
8. You look up → Reyes's fingers are red with crayon wax → he holds up his own note: "IT WAS HIM OR ALL OF US." Maya and DJ look away.
9. The toy walkie Ollie clipped to you crackles → three taps → "I'm still awake." He walked through them awake, eyes shut, because someone told him to.
10. Choice: turn the walkie off and stay safe, or climb down alone for him.
