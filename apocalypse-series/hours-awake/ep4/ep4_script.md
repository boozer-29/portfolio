## 1. The rule this episode makes canon (answers the owner's observation)
**They don't hunt sound. They hunt whoever is awake. Sound is only how an awake person gives themselves away.**
- Eyes shut + silent = they take you for one of them and let you pass (Maya, the nurse, worked it out from watching Ollie; this is how you get through).
- Ollie is different: when he's asleep (or passing as asleep), they don't just ignore him, they **kneel** to him (Ep 1, Ep 2).
- The moment he did something only an awake person does (tapping back to you), they **stood up** and turned on him (his note; the half-standing ring in the basement).
- So: asleep = worshipped; awake = hunted. That is the vote.
- Never explained on screen: *why* they kneel to him. That's the 2 AM question.

## 2. Pre-writing checklist (STORY_CRAFT.md)
- **Ritual:** three taps. Opens the episode (you tap, he answers), returns in the dark (his hand finds yours: tap, tap, tap).
- **Character love (8-15 s):** the reunion. Ollie's silent laugh-cry with both hands over his mouth; his note "MY BЯOTHEЯ CAME" (both R's backwards, the real handwriting, contrasted with Reyes's forgery last episode); he gives you back the dinosaur's tail he'd snapped off and kept ("so you'd have to come back for the rest"). **Maya** gets her human moment: she knew, and she helps anyway.
- **Split-second moment:** the instant you open your eyes in the basement. Then, at the twist, the instant Ollie's eyes close, replayed in slow motion.
- **Guilt, with no real choice:** 91% chose to answer him. The episode reveals the answer is what broke his safety ("He was safe. Until you answered."). And your arrival is what lets him fall asleep. Your love is what's losing him, twice. The alternative (silencing him) was unthinkable, so the viewer had no choice and still feels guilty.
- **Quiet last image:** Ollie asleep against your chest, his hand still curled in "tap" position, never finishing the third tap. Around you, three hundred bowed heads.
- **2 AM line:** "Fifty-four hours, he stayed awake for you. Now you're here..." (he can finally stop).
- **Unanswered on purpose:** why do they kneel to him? And on the next cot, for 6 frames, a clipboard: `SUBJECT 0`, with your name.
- **Thoughts/feelings in narration:** "And your eyes are closed." / "Don't... breathe." / "He was safe. Until you answered." / "Fifty-four hours..."

## 3. Chain of cause and effect
1. Ep 3 vote B → you tap three times → three fast taps back (his joy).
2. Your taps and his are noise → Reyes lunges to take the walkie → Maya grabs his arm and holds him.
3. Maya presses a note into your hand (she knew about Reyes's plan; she's sorry; she tells you how Ollie survived: eyes shut).
4. The only way down is this building's fire escape, into the street where the crackle drew them → you climb down → Reyes, above, hauls the ladder up (no way back for you; you're one more risk to them).
5. At the bottom, faces everywhere → you close your eyes (Maya's advice) → rule one: closed eyes after 53 hours awake is how you fall asleep.
6. Blind: shoulders, breath, the hum → rule two: you hold your breath → they let you pass, because with your eyes shut you're one of them.
7. Across the street, through the shattered lobby (Ep 2), down the stairwell counting steps → your fingertips find raised letters on a door plate: B1.
8. Inside, quiet → a small hand finds yours → three taps → you open your eyes: Ollie, on a cot in an old clinic, ringed by Sleepers. Half the ring kneels, half stands.
9. His notes: "MY BЯOTHEЯ CAME." And: "WHEN I TAPPED BACK THEY STOOD UP." → your answer is what woke them to him.
10. He's 8, awake 54 hours, and his brother is finally holding him → he lets go → his eyes close → the standing half of the ring kneels, then the whole building (the hum drops into one soft chord).
11. Choice: let him sleep (lose him to them, but he's safe) or wake him (keep him, and they rise for both of you).

## 5. Shot-by-shot direction
Style, palette, HUD, captions: CLAUDE.md PART 4. Sleepers are never seen moving.
**New visual language for this episode: EYES-SHUT MODE.** When your eyes are closed, the screen is warm red-black (light through eyelids), with only faint blurred silhouettes when light passes, and soft white "sound rings" drawn where noises happen (a breath, a shuffle, the hum), the way you'd picture them blind. Captions stay. The HUD dims to 30% and shows only NOISE (your breathing) as a big number that you must keep low. This makes the blind walk feel like a game the viewer is playing.

## 8. Where each vote leads (Episode 5, branches never rejoin)
**A · LET HIM SLEEP → Ep 5 "Lullaby" (Hour 60).** You hold him while he sleeps. At dawn he stands up, eyes closed, and walks out of the clinic, and every Sleeper in the city stands with him and follows. He stops at the door and, without opening his eyes, taps your hand twice. The horde walks out of the city behind him, and the streets are safe. You've saved everyone. Except him. Then the walkie in your pocket crackles. It's Reyes: "Where's the kid? ...Good."

**B · WAKE HIM → Ep 5 "Wide Awake" (Hour 55).** You shake him. His eyes open, and three hundred heads rise. The two of you run, awake, through a building that now hunts you both. The only place they won't follow is the one place you shouldn't go: the cot marked `SUBJECT 0`. When you lie on it, the Note starts playing from the clinic speakers, and every Sleeper in the building stops and turns toward you.

## 9. Influences for this episode (structure only)
- **Bird Box:** moving blind through a world where opening your eyes can kill you. We borrow the tension of the blind walk, not the premise: here closing your eyes is what disguises you, and what might put you to sleep.
- **A Quiet Place:** breath as the enemy (the NOISE number you have to keep down).
- **The Last of Us (episode 5, Henry and Sam):** a reunion between brothers that turns into the worst moment, because the younger one has been hiding how close he is to the edge.
