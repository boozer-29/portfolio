## 1. Pre-writing checklist (STORY_CRAFT.md + SERIES_ARC.md)
- **Ritual:** three taps. Ollie's hand rests on yours in "tap" position all night; the third tap never comes. Then, in his sleep, the hand moves again, but to write.
- **Character love (8 s):** the night on the cot: his breathing, his cheek on your chest, his fist still holding the snapped-off dinosaur tail from Ep 4. You hold your own breath so you don't wake him. No horror, just a brother.
- **Fixed milestone (SERIES_ARC):** you are Subject 0. Shown with a file, a photo and the mark behind your ear (Ep 1's plant), and Maya in the photo (she knew because she worked there).
- **Split-second:** his crayon starting to move in his sleep. Then the slow push on the backwards R's, his real handwriting.
- **Guilt, no choice:** A voters chose sleep because it kept him safe. The episode reveals he's trapped and begging to be woken. Waking him meant three thousand of them turning on you both, so it was never a real option. They still feel it.
- **Quiet image:** the empty cot, still warm, the crayon rolling to a stop.
- **2 AM line:** "He's been awake in there. The whole time."
- **Unanswered on purpose:** where is he leading them? (Dawn Camp, SERIES_ARC, revealed later.)

## 2. Chain of cause and effect
1. Ep 4 vote A → you don't wake him → he sleeps on your chest; the ring stays kneeling.
2. You're awake, so you keep your eyes shut (Ep 4 rule) and don't move, all night (rule 1 fight, rule 2 silence).
3. Dawn light through the basement's street-level window warms your eyelids → you open your eyes for the first time in hours.
4. The light falls on the next cot (planted in Ep 4: `SUBJECT 0`) → a file with your name → a photo of you at six with a bandage behind your ear, holding a young nurse's hand → it's Maya. You touch the mark behind your ear.
5. Ollie's hand moves in his sleep → he finds the crayon in his pocket → he writes on the cot sheet: `BЯOTHEЯ... WAKE ME UP` (his backwards R's, so it's really him).
6. He's not resting; he's trapped inside the dream and aware.
7. Before you can decide, he stands up, eyes still closed → every kneeling Sleeper stands at once → he walks to the stairs and they follow, out of the building, out of the street.
8. The city empties behind him (safe), Maya is somewhere up on that roof (answers) → stay, or follow him into the march with your eyes shut.

## 4. Shot-by-shot direction
Style, palette, HUD, captions: CLAUDE.md PART 4. Sleepers never seen moving. Rules shown as red pills (no narration): `RULE #1 NEVER SLEEP` (8.5 s), `RULE #2 NEVER MAKE A SOUND` (11 s), `RULE #3 IF THEY HUM, RUN` (43 s).

### SCENE 1. THE KNEELING (0.0-8.5)
**0.0-1.5** Top-down wide (the most striking frame of the series, so a stranger stops): a basement clinic, rows of old cots, a perfect ring of hundreds of kneeling Sleepers, heads bowed, eyes faintly glowing; in the center, one cot with a small boy (red beanie) asleep on someone's chest (your mustard hoodie). Cold blue except one warm emergency light over the cot. Slow push down. HUD small: `PART 5 · 54:02`. Hook text at 30%: white `THEY KNEEL TO HIM.`, at 1.3 s red `AS LONG AS HE SLEEPS.`
**1.5-4.0** Ground level along the ring: bowed head after bowed head, all facing the cot. Their hum is one low, soft chord, almost a lullaby.
**4.0-6.0** Lime tag `YOU CHOSE: A` pops in the HUD for 3 s. Close: Ollie's sleeping face, a crayon smudge on his cheek.
**6.0-8.5** POV down at him: your arms around him, his fist holding the broken-off dinosaur tail. HUD line 4 amber: `SAFE (ASLEEP)`.

### SCENE 2. THE NIGHT (8.5-15.0) (warm, the love beat)
