## 2. Faisal — character bible (never changes)

- Saudi man, early 30s, tall, broad shoulders, light-brown skin, **sharp short black beard
  with a clean fade line**, thick dark eyebrows, **strong jawline**, calm heavy-lidded dark
  eyes, faint confident half-smile. Never laughs out loud.
- **Clothes:** spotless white thobe (ankle length, buttoned collar), **red-and-white checkered
  shemagh** with a **black double-cord agal**, simple brown leather sandals. Big moments add a
  **black bisht with thin gold trim** over the thobe. Plain silver watch (no logo).
- **Props:** small handle-less Arabic coffee cup (finjan), brass dallah coffee pot, dates on a
  small plate, a peregrine falcon on a leather glove.
- **Signature moves:** (1) **shemagh flick** — throws both shemagh tails back over his
  shoulders before the punchline; (2) **slow sip** of coffee; (3) **the stare** — 1 s of
  silence straight to camera before speaking.
- **Acting:** slow, minimal, unimpressed. Everyone around him overreacts; he never does.

### FAISAL prompt block (paste into every shot that shows him)
```
FAISAL is EXACTLY the man from the reference image: Saudi man in his early 30s, tall, broad-shouldered, light-brown skin, sharp short black beard with a clean fade line, thick dark eyebrows, strong jawline, calm heavy-lidded dark eyes, faint confident half-smile; spotless white thobe with buttoned collar, red-and-white checkered shemagh held by a black double-cord agal (the shemagh is built from a few large faceted polygon planes with a cloth photo texture), plain silver watch, brown leather sandals.
```

**Camera grammar for the mogger beat (every reel):** cut to Faisal **CLOSE-UP from a low angle
(10–15°)**, 85 mm, shallow depth of field, slow push-in 5 %, rim light on the shemagh. Hold 1 s of
silence before the line. Then hard cut to the flex.

**Captions:** Silkscreen Bold dialogue captions (2–4 words, y = 63 %). Hook header in Anton at the
top. **No Arabic script inside generated images** — if Arabic text is wanted, add it in the edit
after a native speaker checks it.

## 7. Rules
- Affectionate, aspirational humour: generosity, coffee, heat, time, family, desert life.
- Never: religion or prayer jokes, real royals or officials, alcohol, sexualised content,
  "oil sheikh" or terrorism stereotypes, real brand logos.
- Clothing accuracy matters: red-and-white shemagh + black agal, white thobe, bisht only for
  formal moments.
- Arabic text only in the edit, checked by a native speaker.
- Same Faisal, same voice, same end line in every reel.
