## 3. The 10 launch reels

Format for every reel: POV hook → older generation expects something normal → Zay reacts like
it's a boss fight → absurd Gen Z "solution" → catchphrase → tee end card.
Runtime 20–30 s. Shot letters = camera grammar in SKILL.md section 3.

### Reel 01 — The Phone Call ✅ RENDERED (`reel01_the_phone_call.mp4`)
POV: *your boss asks you to actually call a client*
1. A wide office — BOSS: "Zay. Call the client."
2. D Zay ECU horror + record scratch — ZAY: "Call? Like… with my voice?"
3. B boss low angle — "Yes. On the phone. Right now."
4. E desk phone insert — *ring ring*
5. C Zay holds receiver like a grenade — "Can I just… email them? Or send a meme?"
6. D boss ECU — "What?!"
7. I top-down, Zay fainted (cold grade) — "Tell my mom… I tried."
8. Golden bedroom, texting — "I quit. Over text." *(whoosh)*
9. F Zay CU — "You met me at a very Gen Z time of my life."
10. L tee card — "Get yours at verygenztime dot com."

### Reel 02 — Five-Year Plan
POV: *mom asks where you see yourself in 5 years*
- Kitchen table, Mom: "So… where do you see yourself in five years?"
- Zay ECU, long pause: "…Is this a trick question?"
- Mom MCU: "A plan, Zay. Goals."
- Zay holds up a phone: "My plan is to be the main character."
- Insert: phone screen-time widget glowing (no readable text). Mom ECU gasp.
- Zay walks out with a smoothie, golden hall light: "Manifesting it as we speak."
- Catchphrase → tee card.

Production:
- **Start print-on-demand** (Printful / Printify / Qikink for India) with heavyweight blanks
  (e.g. Comfort Colors 1717-style or a 240 gsm oversized blank). No inventory risk.
- Move the best seller to **bulk screen printing** (100+ units) once it proves itself; margin
  roughly doubles.
- Send the mockup PNGs to a designer (or vectorize) for print-ready 300 dpi / vector files —
  the AI mockups are for marketing, not for the printer.
- Size run S–XXL; boxy fit — say "size down for regular fit" on the product page.

## 6. Rules
- Never put real brand logos, game titles, celebrities or real people in reels or tees.
- Keep Zay likeable: he's lazy and dramatic, never cruel.
- Same hero, same voice, same end line in every reel. Consistency builds the brand.
