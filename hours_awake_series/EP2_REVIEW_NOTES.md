# HOURS AWAKE EP2 — review notes

## What went wrong with the first EP2 render
- It was built to the wrong narration. The mp3 you attached later (76.6 s) is a different script from the one I
  built to (64.3 s). Visuals did not match what was said (e.g. no echoing footsteps, no glass, no plank jam,
  no 10-hour sky change, "fire escape" shown as a basement stairwell). I listened (transcribed with word
  timestamps) to the new file first, then rebuilt every shot from the spoken words.

## Issues found in my own review pass (and fixes)
1. Captions covered the runner/Ollie in the stair shots -> caption raised for those shots.
2. Fire-escape beanie read as a red blob, then overlapped by caption -> redrawn as a ribbed knit cap with pompom, moved up.
3. Plank bridge from behind was unreadable (wedge) -> redrawn as side elevation with the gap and next roof visible.
4. Vote cards collided with the HUD -> moved down and shrunk.
5. Hands on the stair-shaft rail looked like blobs -> added a rail bar.
6. After the time-lapse the roof, POV and Ollie close-up were still night-lit -> now follow the day state.

## Known weaknesses (not fixed)
- Roof wide shots: the rule-one pill and captions overlap the survivors' bodies (faces stay clear).
- Survivors are small and cramped on the stairwell landing.
- I have not listened to the final mix or watched it in motion; checks were frame sheets and measurements.
