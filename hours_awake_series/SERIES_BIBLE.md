# HOURS AWAKE — series bible (single source of truth; update it at the end of every episode)

Vertical 9:16 interactive horror shorts (~80-85 s). Viewers vote A/B in the comments; the most-voted choice decides the next episode.
Narrator: ElevenLabs "Brian - Deep, Resonant and Comforting" (voice id nPczCjzI2devNBz1zQrb), same voice every episode. Second person ("you").

## The world
A low note played from every phone. One hour later everyone was asleep; at 3:07 they stood up — SLEEPERS: eyes shut, humming, still asleep.
- Rule #1 NEVER SLEEP (one microsleep and you are one of them). Rule #2 NEVER MAKE A SOUND. Rule #3 IF THEY HUM, RUN.
- Revealed in EP4: they do not hunt sound, they hunt whoever is AWAKE; sound is just how an awake person gives themselves away.
  Eyes shut + silent = they let you pass. Asleep (Ollie) = they KNEEL. An awake-only act (tapping back) = they stand and turn on him.
  WHY they kneel to Ollie is never explained (the big mystery).
- Radio: rescue in 7 days (6 after EP2's ten hours).

## Look & format rules
Colour: warm amber = safety/love, cold blue-grey = danger, RED only for alerts, stamps, rules and the red eyelid darkness. HUD top-left every frame (EP n/7, hours-awake clock, SURVIVORS, NOISE dB, SLEEPERS NEARBY, RESCUE IN n DAYS). Karaoke captions (Montserrat 900) never over faces, never in the top 12% or bottom 25%. Rule pills (RULE #n). Fonts: Bebas Neue, Montserrat, JetBrains Mono, Caveat (crayon/handwriting).
Episode shape: first words "Imagine..."; hook in second 1; recap stamp of last vote (e.g. "91% OF YOU..."); mid twist ~60%; big twist 70-85%; >=2 silence drops in real pauses before reveals; vote card A/B with mini-scenes + "COMMENT A OR B · THE MOST VOTED CHOICE DECIDES"; end card "FOLLOW FOR HOUR n".
