# HOURS AWAKE EP1 v2 "THE NOTE": narration to record

Read it in order, one take or beat by beat joined into one mp3. Tags in [brackets] are acting notes, never spoken. `//` means a pause: `//` short about 0.25 s, `///` long about 0.5 s or more. Real silence is better than a breath or a filler word.

## Limits that keep the video inside 85 s
- Speech plus pauses: about 76-78 s in total. The video also needs about 7 s of picture-only moments, such as the plane, the silent street and the final black.
- Do not add music, reverb or background noise. The sound design is mixed on top by me.
- Start the file with about 0.3 s of silence. End it right after "Follow for hour fifty-two."
- MP3 or WAV at 44.1 kHz or higher.

## Script

**B1** [quietly][tense] Imagine you can't sleep. // That's the only... you're alive.

**B2** [calm][ominous] Forty-one hours ago, every phone buzzed. // One low... note.

**B3** [slowly][ominous] One hour later... everyone was asleep. // Drivers. Pilots.

**B4** [serious] At three oh seven... they all stood up. // [whispering] Eyes closed... Humming. // [slowly][darkly] Hunting by sound.

**B5** [softly] You survived... because of your headphones. // [whispers] The note... never reached you.

**B6** [numb][serious] By sunrise, the sirens stopped. /// A plane... fell from the sky.
Leave 1.5 s of silence after "sky". The plane falls silently, then the flash and boom.

**B7a** [serious] Rule number one. // Never sleep. // One microsleep... and you're one of them.

**B7b** [whispers] Rule number two. // Never make a sound. // [serious] It travels a kilometer... in three seconds.

**B7c** [urgent][restrained] Rule number three. // If they hum... [rushed] Run.
Leave 0.8 s of silence after "Run" for the door slam.

**B8** [gently] Four others survived. // A nurse. A soldier. A D J. // [softly] And your brother. // Eight years old.

**B9** [calm] Now... it's hour forty-one. // The radio says: stay awake. // [slowly] Help comes in seven days. /// [very softly][warm] Stay with me.

**B10** [tense] Then... Maya's map lights up. // Thousands of dots. // [whispers] Every one... walking here.
Leave 1 s of silence after "here".

**B11** [whispers] The street goes silent.
Leave 2 s of true silence. Then [slowly] Thousands kneel.
Leave 0.8 s of silence after "kneel".

**B12** [whispering] Their eyes... open. /// [slowly] Every one... looks up. At your window. // [darkly] Not the building. /// [whispering] At you.
Leave 1.2 s of silence after "you".

**B13** [calm][warm] Two ways out. // A... the roof. // B... the basement. // [slowly] Only one... is quiet. // [urgent] Comment A... or B.

**CTA** [softly] Follow for hour fifty-two.

## What I do with your mp3
1. Transcribe it with word timestamps and match every word to this script.
2. Put every cut, caption, HUD change, rule pill and name stamp on the real spoken words.
3. Put each silence drop in the real gaps you leave. The long gaps above are where the picture goes quiet.
4. Mix drone, hum, hits and the heartbeat under your voice, with your voice always the loudest.
5. Render, then check the finished video with frames and measurements, as before.

If your take runs long I will tell you exactly which lines to tighten before I render.
