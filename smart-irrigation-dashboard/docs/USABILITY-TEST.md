# Usability Test Plan

**Product:** Smart Irrigation Decision Dashboard (prototype)
**Method:** Moderated, in-person task-based test with think-aloud, run in the participant's preferred language
**Participants:** 5 smallholder farmers (mix of reading levels; at least 2 who don't read English), plus 1 family member who helps with farming
**Device:** Low-cost Android phone, outdoors or near a window (realistic light)
**Duration:** about 20 minutes per participant

## Before the test
1. Explain the purpose: "We are testing the app, not you. There are no wrong answers."
2. Ask background questions: crop, how they decide when to water today, phone use, preferred language.
3. Open the prototype in the participant's language with numbers **off**.

## Tasks

| # | Scenario (demo button) | Task given verbally | Success = |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dry, no rain | "Should you water your field today? For how long?" | Says yes and gives a time roughly in the range, within 10 s |
| 2 | Dry, no rain | "How sure is the app about this?" | Points to the meter or says "sure" |
| 3 | Rain coming | "Should you water today? Why not?" | Says no and mentions rain |
| 4 | Sensors down | "Is the app sure today? What can you do to help it?" | Says "not sure" and uses Feel the soil |
| 5 | Sensors down | "Is anything broken in your field? What should you do?" | Finds Sensor 3 and says "check battery/wire" |
| 6 | No data at all | "What does the app want you to do?" | Does the hand check and taps the right picture |
| 7 | Any | "Make the app speak to you." | Taps Listen |
| 8 | Dry, no rain | "You have just finished watering. Tell the app." | Taps "I watered today" |

## After the test
- **Single Ease Question** after each task: "How easy was that?" (show 5 faces from sad to happy instead of numbers)
- **Trust question:** "If the app says *Not sure*, would you trust it more or less than an app that is always sure?"
- **Preference:** "Did you use the pictures, the words or the voice the most?"

## Analysis
- Task success rate and median time per task
- List every problem found, rate its severity (0–4, Nielsen scale), and fix the 3 worst first
- Pay special attention to whether participants understand that the **dashed band / dashed border means "not sure"**, since this is the riskiest design assumption
