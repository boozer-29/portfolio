# "What if your afro doubled every day?" — SCRIPT v2 (step 1)

**Premise:** he has a date on Saturday and must look good. Every day he grabs a bigger tool to tame the afro — and every tool loses.
**Running gag:** the tool doubles with the afro (comb → brush → rake → hedge trimmer → leaf blower → mower crew → NASA comb).
**Twist:** his date's afro is twice the size of his.
Stat label `AFRO WIDTH`. ~57 s. Every doubling = BOMBOCLAT + an action beat. Punchlines get a ~0.5 s pause before them and a held reaction after.

---

**TITLE (0–3 s) — Bedroom, morning.** He draws a big red heart around SATURDAY on the wall calendar ("DATE ♥"), then picks his small afro with a comb. Silent title card.
CAMERA: slow push-in from the doorway; calendar sharp in the foreground left, hero behind it at the mirror.

**DAY 1 · 15 cm · FRESHLY PICKED — Bedroom.** He taps the calendar with the comb, winks at the mirror.
HERO (cocky): "Date on Saturday. Easy."
CAMERA: medium front shot, mirror-side; push to his face on "Easy."

**DAY 2 · 30 cm · ATE A HAIRBRUSH — Bedroom.** BOMBOCLAT — the afro pops and blasts things off the side table. He drags a hairbrush through it; the afro swallows the brush, hand and all, then spits his hand back out — and *burps*. Beat. He stares up at it.
HERO (offended): "…Excuse you."
CAMERA: crash zoom on his face for the pop; cut to medium as the brush gets sucked in; hold a 2-shot of face + afro for the burp and the line.

**DAY 3 · 60 cm · HOME TO 3 BIRD FAMILIES — Front yard.** BOMBOCLAT. He drags a garden rake through it and pulls out a whole bird's nest — eggs, a tiny sofa, a tiny TV. A mama bird pops up, furious.
BIRD (indignant): "Excuse me. We have a *lease*."
CAMERA: wide on the yard for the pop; over-the-shoulder close on the nest coming out on the rake; tight on the bird for the line, hero's stunned face soft behind.

**DAY 4 · 1.2 m · BROKE A HEDGE TRIMMER — Barbershop.** BOMBOCLAT — the mirror cracks, the cape blasts off. The barber revs a hedge trimmer, pushes in… it jams, sparks, dies in smoke. Long beat. The barber quietly takes off his apron, puts the shop keys in the hero's hand and walks out.
BARBER (deadpan, walking away): "It's yours now."
CAMERA: low angle on the hero in the chair for the pop; side medium on the trimmer jamming; wide with the hero centred as the barber exits frame right.

**DAY 5 · 2.4 m · WIND RESISTANT — Street.** BOMBOCLAT. He points a leaf blower straight up into the afro and pulls the trigger. The afro doesn't move — *he* does: blown backwards down the street, cartwheeling past stalls and a kid with an ice cream, landing in a heap.
HERO (from the heap, thumbs up): "I'm okay!"
CAMERA: low wide down the street; whip-pan following him as he flies; settle on the heap, his face and thumbs-up in the foreground.

**DAY 6 · 4.8 m · READABLE FROM A HELICOPTER — City + news.** BOMBOCLAT. The city sends a ride-on lawn-mower crew up ladders onto the giant afro. From the air we see they've mowed a crop-circle into it: **HELP**. News studio, the photo on the screen behind.
NEWS ANCHOR (serious): "Experts have confirmed… it does say help."
CAMERA: street-level low angle as mowers climb the ladders; crane up to a straight-down aerial revealing HELP; cut to the anchor with the photo over his shoulder.

**DAY 7 · 9.6 m · HAS ITS OWN MOON — Orbit.** BOMBOCLAT. A rocket launches a giant comb from Earth; it sinks into the afro and jams — and when it's yanked back, the Moon comes out with it, stuck in the teeth. Astronaut's view from the window.
ASTRONAUT (radio, flat): "Houston… we have a tangle."
CAMERA: wide orbit around little-Earth as the rocket climbs; tight on the comb jamming; inside the capsule, astronaut's face with the stuck Moon through the window.

**DAY 8 · 19.2 m · WEARING SATURN — Planets.** BOMBOCLAT. He gives up fighting it. He slides Saturn's rings on like a headband, clips planets in like beads, poses.
HERO (posing, proud): "You know what? It's a look."
CAMERA: low hero angle, slow orbit; planets clicking in one by one; finish on his pose, face clear under the rings.

**SILENCE (0.8 s).** On a tiny table on the Moon: two chairs, a candle, a rose. He checks his watch.
CAMERA: slow push across the candle to his face.

**DAY 9 · 38.4 m · VISIBLE FROM OTHER GALAXIES — The universe.** BOMBOCLAT — one last pop: the afro swallows the starfield and turns into a galaxy. Then the light changes: something *bigger* slides in and eclipses everything. It's his date — her afro twice the size of his, glittering with whole galaxies. She sits down, smiles.
DATE (warm, casual): "Sorry I'm late. Traffic."
HERO: **BOMBOCLAAT?!** (question take)
CAMERA: crash pull-back as his afro becomes a galaxy; the frame darkens from the top as her shadow slides over; reveal wide — two tiny people at the Moon table, her afro filling the sky; end on his face, mouth open.
