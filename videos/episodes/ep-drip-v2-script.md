# "What if your drip doubled every day?" — v2 SCRIPT (step 1, draft)

Rethink after the owner's rule: nothing from earlier videos. No space, no Mum, no pigeons, no anchor, no astronaut, no loop ending, no old shout set.

**Story:** a bouncer turns him away for his £40 charity-shop jacket. He gets back in line every night. His drip doubles until London copies his look, but only the Day 1 jacket, because nobody can afford the rest. **Twist:** on the last night the £40 jacket *is* the dress code, and he's the one person not wearing it.

**New stat display:** no stat block or tier pill. A paper price tag hangs off the top-left corner on a string and swings on every cut. Each day the old price is scribbled out and the new one is handwritten below it, until the tag is a mess of crossed-out numbers.

**Style breaks, one per act (all new to the series):** a nature documentary (letterbox, 16 mm grain, telephoto) · bullet time (frozen rain, 360° orbit) · a courtroom sketch (pencil-on-paper look) · CCTV (green fisheye, timestamp, choppy 12 fps).

**Shouts, only 2, both new deliveries:**
- a whispered BOMBOCLAT that echoes three times round a silent gallery
- a voice-crack squeak at the very end

**Other voices (all new characters):** bouncer (2 lines), documentary narrator (1), judge (1), mugger (1).

---

**TITLE (0–3 s) — Club queue, night.** Neon sign, wet pavement, a velvet rope. He reaches the front and a huge hand lands flat on his chest. Title card.
CAMERA: low behind the rope, pink neon rim light; the hand enters the frame hard.

**DAY 1 · £40 · charity-shop jacket, tag still on** — *"Day 1. A £40 jacket from a charity shop."* / *"The bouncer has questions."*
BOUNCER (deadpan, looking him up and down): "Not tonight, mate. Is that… a curtain?" · HERO: "It's vintage, bredda!"
CAMERA: over the bouncer's shoulder down onto him; his tag swings in the neon.

**DAY 3 · £160 · new white trainers** — *"Day 3. New trainers."* / *"His shadow starts walking like a model."*
Under a streetlamp his shadow on the brick wall breaks away from him: it catwalk-struts, spins and hits a pose while he just walks. He stops; the shadow keeps posing.
HERO: "Oi! Who teach you fi walk so?"
CAMERA: side-on along the wall, long hard shadow; hold the two-shot of him staring at the posing shadow.

**DAY 5 · £640 · a designer jacket** — *STYLE BREAK: nature documentary.* *"Day 5. A trip to the zoo."* / *"The peacock retires."*
NARRATOR (hushed, posh documentary voice): "The male peacock displays his feathers… then sees the competition." The peacock slowly folds its tail and walks into a bush.
HERO (awkward, to the bush): "Sorry, boss."
CAMERA: telephoto through leaves, letterbox bars, 16 mm grain, slow rack focus from the peacock to him.

**DAY 8 · £5,120 · a gold chain** — *"Day 8. He walks past a clothes shop."* / *"The mannequins notice."*
Three shop-window mannequins turn their heads to follow him. One takes off its jacket and drops it on the floor in shame.
HERO (backing away, whispered): "Mi nah like dis."
CAMERA: inside the window behind the mannequins' heads, him on the pavement beyond the glass; the heads turn in sync.

**DAY 10 · £20,480 · a new car** — *"In 2019, a pair of trainers sold for $437,500."* / *"Now he has a man who ties his laces."*
His lace comes undone. A security guard in an earpiece and white gloves kneels and ties it like he's defusing a bomb, then speaks into his sleeve.
CAMERA: macro on the gloved fingers and the lace; tilt up to him checking his nails.

**DAY 12 · £81,920 · two years' wages** — *STYLE BREAK: bullet time.* *"Day 12. It starts to rain."* / *"The rain goes round him."*
Time freezes mid-shower. The camera orbits him 360°, and every raindrop curves away from the jacket. One drop tries it and sizzles into steam. Time snaps back.
HERO (unbothered): "Rain cyaan touch di fit."
CAMERA: full 360° orbit at chest height, drops frozen as streaks; whoosh back to real speed.

**DAY 15 · £655,360 · two houses** — *STYLE BREAK: courtroom sketch.* *"Day 15. He's arrested for stopping traffic."* / *"The judge has one question."*
JUDGE (stern, old): "How do you plead?" · HERO: "Guilty… of being too fresh." Pause. The judge leans over the bench and whispers: "…Where's the jacket from?"
CAMERA: pencil-sketch look on paper; slow push on the judge leaning in.

**DAY 18 · £5.2 million · a private jet** — *STYLE BREAK: CCTV.* *"Day 18. Two men try to rob him."* / *"They tip him instead."*
An alley in green fisheye, timestamp ticking. Two masked muggers approach, freeze and look him up and down. Jump-cut: they walk away clapping while he holds both their wallets.
MUGGER (walking off): "Respect, bro. Respect."
CAMERA: a fixed high corner camera, 12 fps judder, one jump-cut.

**DAY 22 · £84 million · a superyacht** — *"Day 22. He stops in a gallery to tie his shoe."* / *"They won't let him leave."*
He kneels; an attendant drops a velvet rope round him and sticks a little label on the floor. A queue forms. A critic wipes a tear.
HERO (whispered, to himself): *"bomboclat"*, which echoes round the silent hall: *"…claat… claat…"*
CAMERA: a huge white gallery, high wide; he's tiny in the middle; the push-in ends on his frozen face.

**DAY 27 · £2.7 billion · a stadium** — *"Day 27. He pays for crisps with one button."* / *"His change comes in a wheelbarrow."*
Corner shop: he puts one jacket button on the counter. The shopkeeper wheels out a barrow of cash, then adds his own watch to the pile.
CAMERA: low over the counter, the button sparkling in the foreground; the wheelbarrow rolls into frame.

**DAY 30 · £21.5 billion · a small country** — *"Day 30. All of London copies his look."* / *"Only the Day 1 jacket. It's all they can afford."*
Time-lapse of the high street: everyone in the same scruffy £40 jacket with the price tag; a charity-shop queue round the block.
CAMERA: a high wide time-lapse, the crowd blurring past in the same brown jackets.

**END — The club, night (a new angle, not the Day 1 frame).** He arrives glowing in his billion-pound fit; the queue parts. The bouncer is wearing the £40 jacket. Everyone inside is too. The hand lands on his chest again.
BOUNCER: "Sorry, mate. Dress code." He taps his own price tag.
HERO stares down at his own fit… *"bomboclat?"* in a voice-crack squeak. Hard cut to black.
CAMERA: wide from inside the club looking out, a sea of brown jackets; cut to the bouncer's tag; end tight on his face.
