# "What if your laziness doubled every day?" — SCRIPT (step 1, draft)

**Story:** he shares a flat with two people who do chores. He doesn't. Every day his laziness doubles, so every day he builds a bigger, more ridiculous machine to avoid a smaller job. **Flip ending:** a storm cuts London's power. The only machines still working are his string-and-domino contraptions, and he restarts the city without leaving the sofa.

**Laziness doubles, so his steps halve.** The display is a **smartwatch widget** (top-left, new to the series) showing:
- `STEPS TODAY` and the number
- `LAZINESS ×2 DAILY`
- `DAY n`

It buzzes "Time to stand!" and he ignores it every time.

**What makes it spread (MASTER_PROMPT §8c):**
- **Signature sound:** a slow, deadpan Kevin **"Mmm… nah."** before every job. It's one take, reused identically so it becomes the beat, and people can type it back in the comments.
- **Recurring insert:** a **blueprint** (white lines on blue paper) unrolls, then the machine's last piece clicks into place: *tk-tk-tk… DING*. It's the same framing every time.
- **Mock-epic:** huge engineering over tiny jobs.
- **Quotable line:** "Walking is just falling… but slowly."
- **Flip ending:** his laziness saves the city.

**Style breaks (new to the series):** blueprint inserts · a TV infomercial · whispered golf-commentary.

**Shouts (2, new deliveries, Kevin's own voice):**
- **Yawned:** "bombo— *[huge yawn]* —claat".
- **Too lazy to finish:** "bom… bo…", then he falls asleep mid-word. He finishes it ("…claat.") in the very last shot.

**Other voices (all new):**
- Dev, the gym-bro flatmate (2 lines).
- Priya, the tidy flatmate who owns the chore rota (2 lines).
- An infomercial host (1 line).
- A golf commentator (1 line).
- A power-station engineer on the radio (1 line).

**Sets (all new):** shared flat (living room, sofa, hallway, kitchenette) · the street outside · London rooftops · a power station.

---

**TITLE (0–3 s) — The flat, morning.** Priya pins a chore rota to the fridge. Dev jogs past doing lunges. Our hero lies face-down on the sofa under a pizza box. His column on the rota is empty.
HERO (voice-over, flat): "Everyone in this flat does chores. Except me."
CAMERA: slow push across the messy living room, rota sharp in the foreground, sofa soft behind; warm morning light through the blinds.

**DAY 1 · 4,000 steps** — *"Doctors recommend 10,000 steps a day."* / *"He's saving them for later."*
DEV (bouncing on his toes): "Bro. Gym?" · HERO: **"Mmm… nah."** He rolls off the sofa rather than sit up, lands face-first on the rug and stays there.
HERO (into the rug): "Floor's comfy, innit."
CAMERA: low on the rug at his face height; Dev's trainers bounce in the background; the watch buzzes "Time to stand!" in the foreground.

**DAY 2 · 2,000 steps** — *"The remote is two metres away."* / *"So he goes fishing."*
**"Mmm… nah."** He casts a fishing rod from the sofa. Miss: the hook catches Dev's protein shake, which flies across the room and splats on Priya's rota. Second cast: the remote is reeled in.
HERO (calm): "Caught one."
CAMERA: over his shoulder down the rod line to the coffee table; follow the shake's arc in a whip-pan; hard cut to the splat.

**DAY 4 · 500 steps** — *"The light switch is four metres away."* / *"Engineers would call this overkill."*
**First blueprint insert:** "LIGHT-OFF DEVICE MK1" unrolls. Then the real thing: he flicks one domino. Dominoes → a toy car down a ramp → a mousetrap → a string → *DING* → the switch flips and the room goes dark.
HERO (in the dark): "Night."
CAMERA: blueprint top-down; then a low tracking shot racing alongside the chain; the end is a macro on the switch; cut to black and hold for one beat.

**DAY 6 · 125 steps** — *STYLE BREAK: infomercial.* *"It's his turn to wash up."* / *"He'd rather invent something."*
Saturated TV look, a "CALL NOW" banner and a price flashing.
INFOMERCIAL HOST (huge American energy): "Tired of washing up? Introducing… the SINK-O-MATIC!"
A leaf blower, a garden hose and a spinning washing drum blast plates out of the window like frisbees. Dev catches four; the fifth smashes.
HERO: **"bombo— *[huge yawn]* —claat."**
HERO (to camera, deadpan): "Call now. Actually… don't."
CAMERA: locked-off front-on "TV studio" frame of the kitchenette with ring-light glare; cut to the window as plates fly out; a slow-mo plate spins toward the lens.

**DAY 9 · 15.6 steps** — *"Dentists say brush for two minutes."* / *"He outsources it."*
Blueprint insert: "BRUSH-DRONE". A drone with a toothbrush hovers over the sofa and brushes his teeth while he sleeps. It gets confused, flies off and brushes Dev's eyebrows.
HERO (mouth full of foam): "Two minutes. Dentist said."
CAMERA: top-down over the sofa as the drone descends; then a side two-shot of Dev frozen while the drone scrubs his brows.

**DAY 13 · 0.98 steps** — *PAUSE BEAT.* *"He now takes less than one step a day."* / *"Today's is still loading."*
Silence except the watch ticking. He stands up for the first time in the reel. One foot lifts… hovers…
DEV (whispering, desperate): "Bro. It's ONE step."
HERO (dead serious): "Walking is just falling… but slowly." He puts the foot back down. **"Mmm… nah."**
HERO (starting a shout, too lazy to finish): **"bom… bo…"**. His eyes close mid-word and he falls asleep standing up. A snore.
CAMERA: a held, tight close-up on his face, then a slow tilt down to the hovering foot; the only light is a lamp; the watch fills the foreground.

**DAY 16 · 0.12 steps** — *STYLE BREAK: golf commentary.* *"The pizza is at the door."* / *"The door is eleven metres away."*
Hushed TV-golf framing with a scoreboard graphic: "THE SOFA OPEN · HOLE 16".
GOLF COMMENTATOR (whispering): "He's going for the doorbell… from the sofa. The audacity."
A telescopic robot arm made of extending tongs and umbrellas snakes down the hallway, takes the pizza and flicks a coin back down a chute to the driver.
HERO: "Keep the change. It's on its way."
CAMERA: long lens straight down the hallway (TV golf framing), the arm extending toward the lens; reverse shot of the driver catching the coin.

**DAY 20 · 0.0076 steps** — *"Putting on socks takes about nine seconds."* / *"He builds a factory to save them."*
The whole flat is now an assembly line. A conveyor carries him from bed to sofa. Robot claws put his socks on, his hoodie on and a crisp in his mouth. Priya rides past on a conveyor too, holding her rota, furious.
PRIYA: "Your name is STILL not on this rota."
HERO: **"Mmm… nah."**
CAMERA: a wide crane shot rising over the flat, which now looks like a factory floor (sparks, arms, belts); land on him gliding past, eyes closed.

**DAY 25 · 0.00024 steps** — *"The chippy is 300 metres away."* / *"So he strings the whole city together."*
Blueprint insert: "LONDON MK1". A web of string, pulleys and dominoes now runs out of his window, over the rooftops and across London. Chips arrive by zipline. Neighbours start using his strings too.
HERO: "Why walk when London can do it?"
CAMERA: a drone shot pulling back out of his window, along a single string over rooftops, revealing the whole city laced with it at dusk.

**DAY 30 · 0.0000075 steps** — *THE FLIP.* *"A storm cuts the power across London."* / *"Every machine stops. Except his."*
Blackout, street by street. Sirens. Then on the radio:
ENGINEER (crackly radio, panicking): "The backup switch is twelve kilometres away… and nobody can get here!"
Silence drop (0.6 s). His hand reaches out from the sofa and tugs one string.
The biggest chain reaction of the reel runs from his window, across the rooftops (callback to the Day 4 dominoes) and into the power station, where the last domino tips the giant lever: **DING**. The lights come back on street by street, racing back toward the flat.
CAMERA: the hand-tug in macro; then fast tracking shots chasing the chain over London; the lever in a low hero angle; the city lighting up from a high wide shot, ending on his window.

**END — The flat.** Priya writes his name on the rota at last: "SAVED LONDON ✓". Dev bursts in.
DEV: "Bro! The King wants to give you a medal! You have to go to the palace!"
HERO (eyes closed): **"Mmm… nah."**
A conveyor rolls the medal in and drops it onto his chest. He wakes for half a second and finishes the shout from Day 13: **"…claat."** He goes back to sleep. Cut to black.
CAMERA: a wide from the doorway (the same angle as the title, now spotless and lit); push in to a tight two-shot of the medal landing and his face.
