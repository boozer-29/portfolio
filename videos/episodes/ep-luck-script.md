# "What if your luck doubled every day?" — SCRIPT (approved 2026-11-25; built in videos/luck-src)

**Story:** he's the unluckiest man in town, and his smug neighbour Marcus always wins. Every day his luck doubles. Bad luck has to go somewhere, so it bounces off him onto Marcus. People start following him and copying everything he does.
**Flip ending:** he wins the lottery jackpot, but the whole country copied his numbers. His share is 64p. That's exactly the price of the last sausage roll, the one Marcus took from him in the first shot.

**Already used (banned here):**
- **Settings:** bedroom, mirror, flat, street chases, barbershop, club, gallery, court, space, news studio.
- **Characters:** Mum, pigeons, news anchor, astronaut, flatmates.
- **Style breaks:** B&W montage, Frutiger, nature doc, bullet time, sketch, CCTV, blueprint, infomercial, golf.
- **Stat displays:** stat block, price tag, smartwatch.
- **Shouts:** confused, stutter, long, resigned, whispered, voice-crack, yawned, split-asleep.
- **Endings:** loop-back, bigger date, dress code, saves the city.

Nothing below repeats any of these.

**Stat display (new): a slot-machine widget** in the top-left safe area.
- `LUCK` label, three reels, `1 IN n` (the odds he beats today), a comparison line, `DAY n`, and a red pill `×2 DAILY`.
- The reels spin on every new day and land:
  - Day 1: lemon · lemon · skull
  - Then cherries, then bells
  - Finally 7 · 7 · 7, which spins forever.

**Viral kit (§8c):**
- **Signature sound + POV insert:** every day opens on the same macro top-down shot of his thumb flicking a coin onto the back of his hand. **TING!** Heads. (Day 1 is a dull *thk*: tails.)
- **Hook in the first second:** "Everybody wins something. Except me."
- **Quotable line:** Marcus's smug **"Unlucky, mate."** comes back reversed in the last shot.
- **Mock-epic:** god-tier luck, spent on a 64p sausage roll.

**Music (owner rule, MASTER_PROMPT 9.18):**
- Proposed track: **AURA** (phonk, the "power" track). The drop lands on the first TING (Day 2).
- It plays at full presence the whole reel: never muffled, ducked only 5–6 dB under lines, and only dipped (never cut) for the Day 13 pause.

**Shouts (2, both new deliveries, Kevin's own voice):**
- **Laughing fit:** "Bombo— *[can't stop laughing]* —claat!"
- **Choir-sung:** a hallelujah "Bombo-claaaaat" as the lottery balls match.

**Other voices (all new):**
- **Marcus**, the smug neighbour: 4 short lines.
- **Bakery lady:** 1 line.
- **Arcade kid:** 1 line.
- **Racing commentator:** 1 line.
- **Casino pit boss:** 1 line.
- **Lottery TV host:** 1 line.
- **Background voices** in every scene: queue, honking drivers, racegoers, casino crowd, pub crowd.

**Sets (all new):** bakery · windy bridge · rush-hour junction · seaside arcade · Marcus's driveway · racecourse · launderette · casino · pub.

Every beat has a **READ:** line: what a viewer understands with the sound off and no English.

**TITLE (0–3 s) — Bakery, rainy morning.**
One sausage roll is left in the glass counter. Marcus (white polo, gelled hair, gold watch) is in front of the hero. He takes it, bites it and turns round with a smirk.
MARCUS: "Unlucky, mate."
HERO (voice-over, starts at 0.3 s): "Everybody wins something. Except me."
READ: the last one gets taken right in front of him; the smirk says it all.
CAMERA: low over the counter glass, the single roll sharp in the foreground; Marcus's hand takes it; rack focus to the hero's sad face behind; rain on the window.

**DAY 1 · 1 IN 2 · ≈ A COIN FLIP** — *"Day 1. He loses a coin flip."* / *"Against himself."*
POV insert: the coin lands **tails** (*thk*). Then his toast slips off the plate, flips in slow motion and lands butter-side down on his new white trainer.
HERO (flat): "Course it did."
READ: tails, toast, butter on the shoe.
CAMERA: macro top-down on the hand; then a low side shot following the toast's flip down to the shoe, ending on a butter-splat close-up.

**DAY 2 · 1 IN 4 · ≈ 2 HEADS IN A ROW** — *"Day 2. The wind picks a side."* / *"His."*
**TING!** Heads. The music drops. On a windy bridge, people chase their hats. A £5 note tumbles down the bridge, past everyone, and slaps flat onto his face.
HERO (peeling it off, stunned): "Five pound? For ME?"
READ: a note flies past everyone else and lands on him.
CAMERA: long lens down the bridge, the note fluttering toward the lens; whip to his face as it lands; hold on his eyes over the note.

**DAY 3 · 1 IN 8 · ≈ 3 HEADS IN A ROW** — *"Day 3. Rush hour."* / *"Every light turns green for him. Only him."*
He walks up to a huge junction. Every pedestrian light flips to the green man as he arrives, one after another down the road, like dominoes of green. Hundreds of cars stack up honking.
DRIVER (background, leaning out): "Oi! Seriously?!"
HERO (strolling, hands in pockets): "I never even pressed the button."
READ: green men light up just for him; a wall of angry cars.
CAMERA: high over the junction looking down the road, the green lights rippling away from camera; drop to a low tracking shot beside him with the car grilles behind.

**DAY 5 · 1 IN 32 · ≈ A CLAW MACHINE WIN** — *"Claw machines usually grip properly about once every 30 goes."* / *"First go."*
Seaside arcade, neon. He drops in one coin. The claw closes, lifts the **entire pile** of teddies in one giant clump, and the machine spits them all out until he's buried to the chest.
KID (pointing, outraged): "Mum! He took ALL of them!"
HERO (buried in teddies, laughing): **"Bombo— hahaha —claat!"**
READ: one coin, every prize, a furious kid.
CAMERA: inside the glass looking up at the claw (neon bokeh); cut to a wide as the teddy avalanche pours out; end low on his face poking out of the pile.

**DAY 7 · 1 IN 128 · ≈ 7 HEADS IN A ROW** — *"Day 7. Bad luck still exists."* / *"It just goes next door."*
Marcus's driveway: he polishes his shiny red convertible; the hero walks past on the pavement. A bus hits a puddle and a wall of water rises toward the hero. It **bends around him** like a tunnel and lands entirely on Marcus.
MARCUS (dripping, sponge in hand): "Un… lucky."
READ: the water curves around the hero; Marcus is soaked.
CAMERA: low side-on at puddle level, both men in frame (hero near, Marcus 3 m behind); slow-mo as the wave arcs over and around the hero; Marcus's dripping face to finish.

**DAY 9 · 1 IN 512 · ≈ A 500-TO-1 OUTSIDER** — *STYLE BREAK: TV horse racing* (racing graphics, odds ticker, a commentator).
*"Day 9. He bets £2 on the worst horse in the race."* / *"500 to 1."*
His horse, NO CHANCE, is a tiny scruffy pony. Marcus waves his ticket for the favourite. Three racegoers behind the hero quietly copy his bet. The favourite stops dead to eat a fan's sandwich; every other horse trips over it; NO CHANCE trots past them all.
COMMENTATOR (frantic): "And the favourite has stopped… for a sandwich!"
Marcus tears his ticket in half. The three copiers cheer.
READ: the scrappy pony wins while the big horses pile up; the people who copied him win too.
CAMERA: TV-style long-lens tracking alongside the rail; a finish-line insert as the pony noses ahead; cut to Marcus tearing his ticket, the hero and three copiers celebrating behind.

**DAY 13 · 1 IN 8,192 · PAUSE BEAT** — *"A coin lands on its edge about once in 6,000 flips."* / *(no punchline)*
A launderette at night, fluorescent hum, drums spinning. The POV insert again: he flicks the coin… and it lands **on its edge** on the back of his hand and stays there. Total stillness. The music dips but never stops.
HERO (quiet, staring): "…Okay. Now I'm scared."
READ: an impossible standing coin and his frozen face.
CAMERA: the same macro framing as every day (so the change hits), held; then a slow push into his face, lit by the washing-machine windows; no cut for 3 s.

**DAY 20 · 1 IN 1 MILLION · ≈ A ROYAL FLUSH** — *"A royal flush: 1 in 649,740 hands."* / *"He gets five in a row."*
A casino, green felt, chandeliers. Top-down card reveals: royal flush, royal flush, royal flush. Behind him, a crowd (now about 30) copies every bet. The chip towers on the table grow taller than the dealer. A pit boss walks up and slides **every** chip toward him.
PIT BOSS (sweating, polite): "Sir. Please. Take it all and leave."
READ: the same perfect hand again and again; the casino begs him out.
CAMERA: top-down on the felt for the card flips (one more each time, same framing); then low past the chip towers to the crowd copying behind him; the pit boss enters from the side, never between the camera and the hero's face.

**DAY 23 · 1 IN 8.4 MILLION · ≈ 23 HEADS IN A ROW** — *"Only one person has ever been confirmed hit by a meteorite."* / *"Today, it's a car."*
Marcus's driveway again (callback to Day 7). Marcus proudly shows off the convertible's new paint. The hero walks past, now followed by a crowd of hundreds. A streak of fire crosses the sky and flattens the convertible into a pancake. The hero, one metre away, doesn't flinch.
MARCUS (tiny, broken): "Unlucky…"
HERO (patting his shoulder): "Look on the bright side. You've got a meteorite."
READ: the fireball misses the hero and destroys exactly one thing, Marcus's pride and joy.
CAMERA: low wide with the car centre-frame and the sky above; the meteor streak enters top-left; crash zoom and shake on impact; dust clears on the two men, the hero calm, Marcus holding a sponge.

**DAY 26 · 1 IN 67 MILLION · ≈ THE LOTTO JACKPOT** — *STYLE BREAK: a 1990s VHS lottery draw* (scanlines, tracking wobble, cheesy set).
*"Lotto jackpot: 1 in 45 million."* / *"By now, the whole country copies his numbers."*
Insert: he fills a ticket with his eyes shut. Then a montage: thousands of people in a stadium-sized queue filling in the same numbers, and ticket printers jamming. In a packed pub everyone watches the TV. The balls drop and match one by one; the whole pub rises with every ball.
HERO (on his knees, choir-sung): **"Bombo-claaaaaaat!"** Confetti.
LOTTERY HOST (TV, beaming): "This week's jackpot is shared by thirty-one million winners!"
Insert: his phone, "YOU WON: £0.64". The whole pub looks at its phones too.
HERO (deadpan): "Sixty-four... pennies." (changed from "pence": Kevin's "pence" was heard as "pins")
READ: everyone has the same ticket, so everyone wins, so nobody wins: 64p on the phone.
CAMERA: macro on the ticket and pen; the VHS-look TV draw with balls tumbling; a wide pub shot from behind the bar, every arm going up; the phone insert held long enough to read; end on his face.

**END — The bakery, rainy morning** (the title set, from a new angle).
One sausage roll left. The price card says **64p**. Marcus is in front, soaked, singed, wallet empty and turned inside out, moths flying out. The hero slides his 64p across the counter.
BAKERY LADY: "Last one, love."
He takes it, bites it and turns to Marcus.
HERO: **"Unlucky, mate."**
Final POV insert: he flips the coin he got as change. It lands on its edge on the counter and spins, and spins… cut to black on the TING.
READ: the exact same moment as the opening, reversed.
CAMERA: from behind the counter this time (the reverse of the title), the roll and price card sharp in the foreground; a two-shot for "Unlucky, mate." with both faces clear; then the macro coin spin.
