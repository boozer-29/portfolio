# "What if your Wi-Fi range doubled every day?" — SCRIPT (step 1)

Stat block: `WI-FI RANGE` · number with unit · comparison line · `DAY n` · red pill `×2 EVERY DAY` · status pill HOME → STREET → CITY → COUNTRY → PLANET → SOLAR SYSTEM → GALACTIC → EVERYWHERE.
Captions: two beats per step (setup / punchline), Lora italic under the stat block. ~75 s, ~4 s per step. Music bed (TIKI TIKI), SFX on every consequence.
**Signature visual:** every doubling, a glowing Wi-Fi ring sweeps out from the router across the scene — everything it touches lights up and connects (screens wake, phones ping).
**Running gag:** "one bar in the bathroom" opens and closes the episode (loop). **Callback:** the neighbour's Peppa Pig comes back from another star.
**Shouts (4, all different):** confused "bomboclat?" · stuttered "bombo… bombo… bomboclat!" · long "BOMBOOOOO—" hard-cut · resigned "hmm… bomboclat".
**Voice:** one line — the internet provider's voicemail.

**DAY 23 · 41,943 km · PAST THE SATELLITES · [PLANET]** — *"Day 23. Past the GPS satellites."* / *"Every map app now says 'Turn left at his house.'"*

**DAY 27 · 671,089 km · THE MOON HAS FULL BARS · [PLANET]** — *"Day 27. The Moon now has full bars."* / *(beat)*

**DAY 36 · 344 million km · MARS · [SOLAR SYSTEM]** — *"Day 36. Mars connects."* / *"Ping: 25 minutes. The rover still blames lag."*
A rover on red dust, a tiny loading wheel spinning on its camera mast, dust devil behind.
CAMERA: low on the Martian ground, the rover rolling past, dust in the low sun.

**DAY 43 · 44 billion km · PASSES VOYAGER 1 · [SOLAR SYSTEM]** — *"Day 43. Voyager 1 took 49 years to get this far."* / *"You took 43 days."*
The ring overtakes the lonely probe in deep space.
CAMERA: the probe small against stars, the ring sweeping through and past it, the Sun a tiny bright dot behind.

**DAY 53 · 4.76 light-years · THE NEAREST STAR · [GALACTIC]** — *"Day 53. The nearest star system has full bars."* / *"If anyone lives there, they're watching your Netflix too."*
On an alien planet, a blob creature on a sofa: "Continue watching: Peppa Pig — Season 4".
CAMERA: a two-sun sky through the alien window, the creature lit by the TV; same framing as Day 3's TV shot.

**DAY 72 · 2.5 million light-years · ANDROMEDA · [GALACTIC]** — *"Day 72. Andromeda connects."* / *"Your 'hi' arrives in 2.5 million years. They left you on read."*
His phone: a chat bubble "hi" with a tiny "Read" under it.
CAMERA: the spiral galaxy in full, slow rotation; insert on the phone screen.

**DAY 87 · 82 billion light-years · EVERYTHING · [EVERYWHERE]** — *"Day 87. The entire observable universe is on your Wi-Fi."*
The ring sweeps past the last galaxies to the edge of everything. Half a second of silence.
CAMERA: crash pull-back through galaxy clusters to the cosmic web, all glowing cyan.

**END — Bathroom.** *"And still…"* / *"One bar in the bathroom."*
Back on the toilet, phone held up: one bar, the wheel spinning. Shout: **"hmm… bomboclat."** Loops to the Day 1 shot.
CAMERA: identical framing to Day 1 (the loop), the bulb flickers once.
